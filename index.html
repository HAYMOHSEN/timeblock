TimeBlock HUD – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

TimeBlock HUD is a Windows app made by Jotech. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app uses

  • Calendar links you add. When you paste a calendar link (an .ics address), TimeBlock HUD downloads that calendar to show your events on the desktop.
  • Blocks you create in the app, and your settings.
  • Your current wallpaper. The app reads your wallpaper picture so it can draw the timeline on top of it.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on your PC. Calendar copies, blocks and settings are saved in the app's own data folder. In "Wallpaper picture" mode, the app also saves pictures of your desktop timeline and a copy of your original wallpaper in your Pictures folder, under "TimeBlock HUD".

Jotech does not run a server for this app and never receives your calendar, your blocks or your settings.

Network use

The app connects only to the calendar addresses you add, to download your calendars. It does not send your data anywhere else.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking or usage reports.
  • No selling or sharing of information with anyone.

Your control

  • Remove a calendar in Settings → Calendars; its saved copy is deleted.
  • Delete blocks in Settings → Your blocks.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data folder. Pictures saved in Pictures\TimeBlock HUD can be deleted at any time.
